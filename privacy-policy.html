隐私政策

Privacy Policy

一、引言

野猪睡眠（以下简称"本App"）高度重视用户的隐私保护。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。

请您在使用本App前仔细阅读本政策。如您不同意本政策的任何内容，请停止使用本App。

二、我们收集的信息

【您提供的信息】

  • 昵称：您可自愿设置个人昵称，用于在App内展示。

【我们自动收集的信息】

  • 睡眠数据：包括入睡时间、起床时间、睡眠时长，通过Apple Watch（HealthKit）或iPhone传感器自动采集。
  • 设备信息：设备型号、iOS版本号，用于兼容性优化和故障排查。
  • App使用数据：页面访问、功能使用频率（匿名统计，不含个人身份信息）。

【我们不收集的信息】我们不会收集以下信息：

  • 您的真实姓名、身份证号、电话号码、家庭住址
  • 您的精确地理位置
  • 您的通讯录、照片、短信内容
  • 您的Apple ID密码或支付信息
  • 通过麦克风录制的内容（本App不启用麦克风权限）

三、信息的使用

我们使用您的信息仅用于以下目的：

  • 睡眠数据：生成睡眠评级、AI评价、月度统计
  • 昵称：个人中心展示
  • 设备信息：兼容性适配、Bug修复

我们不会将您的个人信息用于任何广告推送或商业营销目的。

四、信息的存储与保护

【存储方式】

  • 本地存储：所有睡眠数据、评级记录均存储在您的iPhone本地设备中。
  • iCloud同步：如您开启iCloud，数据将通过Apple iCloud进行加密同步，以便您在多台设备间访问。

【数据安全】

  • 我们不运营后端服务器，您的数据不会上传至我们的服务器。
  • iCloud同步由Apple提供，遵循Apple的隐私和安全标准。
  • 我们采用行业标准的安全措施保护数据，但无法保证绝对安全。

【数据保留】您的数据将保留在您的设备上，直至您：

  • 手动删除App（本地数据将被清除）
  • 通过iOS设置关闭iCloud同步

五、信息的共享

我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租或共享您的个人信息。

仅在以下情形下，我们可能披露您的信息：

  • 法律法规要求或政府机关的合法要求
  • 保护我们的合法权益或用户安全所必需
  • Apple作为技术服务商提供iCloud同步服务（数据由Apple处理）

六、HealthKit 数据说明

【数据读取】如您使用Apple Watch，本App将通过Apple HealthKit读取您的睡眠分析数据（Sleep Analysis）。

【数据使用限制】

  • 本App仅读取HealthKit中的睡眠数据，不会写入或修改您的健康数据。
  • 我们不会将HealthKit数据用于广告或数据销售。
  • HealthKit数据不会离开您的设备或iCloud环境。

【权限管理】您可随时在iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 健康 → 野猪睡眠」中关闭HealthKit数据访问权限。

七、您的权利

【访问与更正】您可以在本App内随时查看您的睡眠记录、评级和评论内容。

【删除权】您有权要求我们删除您的数据。由于数据存储在本地，您可通过以下方式删除：

  • 删除本App（清除所有本地数据）
  • 在设置中关闭iCloud同步

八、儿童隐私

本App不面向13岁以下儿童。我们不会故意收集13岁以下儿童的个人信息。如您发现我们收集了此类信息，请立即联系我们删除。

九、第三方服务

本App使用以下第三方服务：

  • HealthKit（Apple Inc.）— 读取Apple Watch睡眠数据
  • iCloud（Apple Inc.）— 数据跨设备同步

上述服务的隐私保护由Apple负责，请参阅Apple的隐私政策。

十、隐私政策的变更

我们保留随时修改本隐私政策的权利。修改后的政策将在本App内公布，自公布之日起生效。重大变更将通过App内通知告知您。

1. Introduction

AnimalSleep (the "App") takes user privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information.

Please read this policy carefully before using the App. If you do not agree with any part of it, please stop using the App.

2. Information We Collect

[Information you provide]

  • Nickname: You may voluntarily set a nickname shown within the App.

[Information we collect automatically]

  • Sleep data: Including bedtime, wake-up time and sleep duration, collected automatically via Apple Watch (HealthKit) or iPhone sensors.
  • Device information: Device model and iOS version, used for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • App usage data: Page visits and feature usage frequency (anonymous statistics, no personal identifiers).

[Information we do NOT collect] We do not collect:

  • Your real name, ID number, phone number or home address
  • Your precise geolocation
  • Your contacts, photos or messages
  • Your Apple ID password or payment information
  • Content recorded via microphone (the App does not request microphone permission)

3. Use of Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • Sleep data: generating sleep ratings, AI evaluations and monthly statistics
  • Nickname: display in your profile
  • Device information: compatibility and bug fixes

We do not use your personal information for advertising or commercial marketing.

4. Storage & Protection

[Storage]

  • Local storage: All sleep data and ratings are stored locally on your iPhone.
  • iCloud sync: If you enable iCloud, data is encrypted and synced via Apple iCloud so you can access it across devices.

[Security]

  • We do not operate backend servers; your data is never uploaded to our servers.
  • iCloud sync is provided by Apple and follows Apple's privacy and security standards.
  • We adopt industry-standard security measures, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

[Retention] Your data remains on your device until you:

  • Delete the App manually (local data is cleared)
  • Disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with any third party.

We may only disclose your information when:

  • Required by laws or legitimate requests from government authorities
  • Necessary to protect our legal rights or user safety
  • Apple provides iCloud sync as a technical service provider (data processed by Apple)

6. HealthKit Data

[Reading data] If you use an Apple Watch, the App reads your sleep analysis data through Apple HealthKit.

[Usage limits]

  • The App only reads sleep data from HealthKit and never writes or modifies your health data.
  • We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or data sales.
  • HealthKit data never leaves your device or iCloud environment.

[Permission management] You can disable HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → AnimalSleep.

7. Your Rights

[Access & correction] You can view your sleep records, ratings and comments in the App at any time.

[Deletion] You have the right to request deletion of your data. Since data is stored locally, you can delete it by:

  • Deleting the App (clears all local data)
  • Disabling iCloud sync in Settings

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you find that we have collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • HealthKit (Apple Inc.) — reading Apple Watch sleep data
  • iCloud (Apple Inc.) — cross-device data sync

Privacy for these services is governed by Apple. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Revised terms will be published in the App and take effect upon publication. Significant changes will be notified via in-app notifications.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions or complaints about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via:

  • Email: 13039475@qq.com
  • Feedback: App Settings → Feedback
